# Ruka Folder Architecture

**Status:** Proposed and approved as a target structure  
**Source:** User-provided Drive audit and subsequent confirmation  
**Important:** This file documents the target structure. It does not claim that all folders have been created in Google Drive.

## Structure

```text
RUKA CREATIVE WORKSPACE
├── 00 — INBOX
├── 01 — BRAND & IDENTITY
├── 02 — PROJECT MANAGEMENT
├── 03 — DESIGN & CREATIVE ASSETS
│   ├── Templates & Presets
│   └── Stock & Third-party
├── 04 — CONTENT & COPYWRITING
├── 05 — RESEARCH & REFERENCES
├── 06 — DOCUMENTATION
│   └── SOP & Tool Guides
├── 07 — AI KNOWLEDGE BASE
│   ├── Prompts & Tools
│   └── Logs & Apps
├── 08 — ADMIN & FINANCE
└── 09 — ARCHIVE
```

## Folder responsibilities

### 00 — INBOX
Temporary landing zone for newly received or created files that have not yet been classified. Review regularly; do not use as permanent storage.

### 01 — BRAND & IDENTITY
Ruka's own identity system and brand documentation: logo masters, color systems, typography, brand guidelines, tone of voice, and approved identity assets.

### 02 — PROJECT MANAGEMENT
Client and project records. Suggested structure:
```text
02 — PROJECT MANAGEMENT/
└── CLIENT_CODE/
    └── CLIENT_PROJECT/
        ├── 01 — Brief
        ├── 02 — Planning
        ├── 03 — Working Files
        ├── 04 — Deliverables
        └── 05 — Handover
```
Do not create client folders until client names and project records are verified.

### 03 — DESIGN & CREATIVE ASSETS
Working design files, reusable templates, presets, mockups, illustration, stock assets, and production resources. Separate third-party assets from original Ruka work and record license status.

### 04 — CONTENT & COPYWRITING
Content calendars, captions, scripts, copy decks, campaign messaging, and approved text.

### 05 — RESEARCH & REFERENCES
Research, inspiration, competitor references, third-party catalogs, and reference material. Keep source attribution and usage restrictions.

### 06 — DOCUMENTATION
SOPs, tool guides, onboarding instructions, production specifications, and internal process documentation.

### 07 — AI KNOWLEDGE BASE
Prompts, custom assistant instructions, Gemini Gems, AI tools, application notes, and logs. Keep reusable prompts separate from platform-generated logs.

### 08 — ADMIN & FINANCE
Invoices, estimates, contracts, payment records, tax-related documents, and administrative records. Restrict access to sensitive records.

### 09 — ARCHIVE
Inactive or completed project records and historical folders. Archive rather than delete; preserve original ownership and provenance.

## Migration rules

- Prefer shortcuts for shared files owned by other accounts.
- Do not move platform-managed folders without confirming impact.
- Do not move or rename original files until ownership, dependencies, and licensing are understood.
- Keep an inventory of source location and proposed destination.
- Treat all migration actions as pending until separately authorized and executed.
